## 🎭 Use Cases & Recommendations

### Choose **Dark Theme** if you:
- 👨‍💻 Are a developer/programmer
- 🌃 Prefer dark mode aesthetics
- 🎯 Want to stand out boldly
- ⚡ Like modern, energetic vibes
- 🔶 Love warm accent colors

### Choose **Light Theme** if you:
- 💼 Want a corporate/professional look
- 📄 Prioritize readability
- 🤝 Are applying to traditional companies
- 🔵 Prefer calm, trustworthy vibes
- ☀️ Like bright, clean designs

### Choose **Minimal Pro** if you:
- 🎨 Are a designer or creative
- ✨ Love minimalist aesthetics
- 🟣 Want a unique purple accent
- 📐 Believe "less is more"
- 🧘 Prefer calm, focused layouts
